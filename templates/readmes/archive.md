# archive — finished batches

<!-- managed by `novadev deploy`. -->

Fully-recorded batches move here from `../todos/`, grouped by year
(`archive/2026/2026-08-16_1/…`). Nothing inside is edited after the move — this is
the project's development history, browsable and git-tracked.

Move batches with `novadev archive <batch>` or `novadev archive --done` (moves
every batch whose todos all carry a terminal record).
