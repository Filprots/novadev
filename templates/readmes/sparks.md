# sparks — raw ideas

<!-- managed by `novadev deploy`. -->

The idea shelf: unshaped thoughts, fragments, "what if"s — anything not yet
concrete enough to be a todo brief or a postponed item. Free-form files; no
required format. When a spark matures, it graduates into a `todos/` brief (or a
`postponed/` item if it has a known trigger) and the spark file is deleted.
